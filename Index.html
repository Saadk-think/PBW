QUESTIONS / ANSWERED

Good questions.
Great beginnings.

Every author's journey looks different. Here's what you might want to know before we start.

Can you help me if I only have an idea?+

Absolutely. A consultation helps us understand your idea, what you've already developed and which writing services may be appropriate.

Do I need a finished manuscript?+

No. We work with ideas, outlines, drafts and completed manuscripts. The right service depends on where you are in the process.

How much does writing or publishing cost?+

Costs vary based on manuscript length, complexity, timelines and selected services. We'll prepare a project specific scope and quote after learning more about your book.

How long does a book project take?+

Timing depends on your manuscript, the services involved and feedback cycles. We'll discuss a realistic schedule before starting.

Can you help with publishing platforms?+

Yes, we can discuss formatting and publishing setup for appropriate print and digital platforms. Platform eligibility and requirements are confirmed for your specific project.

Who owns the rights to my book?+

Rights and usage terms should be clearly documented in the service agreement for your project. We can review those terms during your consultation.

Can you help promote my book too?+

Yes. Available support includes book marketing strategy, author websites and other promotional services tailored to the audience you want to reach.

What happens after I get in touch?+

We'll review your project details and discuss the services, goals, scope and next steps that make sense for your book.
